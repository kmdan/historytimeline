# Feature ideas from comparable projects (research, 2026-10-12)

Survey of "Earth / history through time" visualisations and what is worth borrowing. Marks: "(memory)" = from the
researcher's knowledge, not re-checked; Chronas and Ancient Earth were described from press/reviews (sites not fetched).

## Projects and their notable features
- **Chronas** (chronas.org): Wikipedia side panel per realm; marker filters; (memory) colour by ruler/culture/religion/population, "epics" (linked event sequences), realm area/population charts, user corrections.
- **GeaCron** (geacron.com): year box + step buttons ±1/10/100; regional embed; paid 3D globe, KML/shapefile export.
- **Ancient Earth** (dinosaurpictures.org/ancient-earth): PALEOMAP globe 750 Ma; "jump to milestone" dropdown; address search → your town's pin + nearby fossils.
- **climatearchive.org**: enter a present-day location + age → reconstructed position and climate plot; layer toggles; "past analogues" for future climates.
- **OpenHistoricalMap**: corner time slider that animates over a chosen range; remembers last date.
- **Running Reality**: citations on every fact; JPEG of a moment, MP4/GIF of a span; JS embed.
- **Our World in Data**: map + play button; click country → time series; table sorted by change between two years; download PNG/SVG/CSV; sources tab; full state in URL.
- **Histography**: every Wikipedia event as a dot, zoom decades→Ma, category filters, curated stories.
- **ChronoZoom** (retired): narrated guided tours, exhibits, five fixed big-history bands.
- **TimeMaps**: world → regional drill-down, map + short article per step.
- **Seshat World Map**: play with years-per-second rate, colour polities by a variable, opacity slider, select polities + "hide unselected".
- **Euratlas Periodis**: one map per century, gazetteer, sovereign vs dependent colouring.
- **Old Maps Online**: map view + date slider filters a list of 500k scanned historic maps.
- **Google Earth Voyager**: guided story tours, knowledge cards, random jump, shareable postcards.
- **NASA Worldview**: compare dates A/B by swipe, opacity fade or circular "spy" lens; animation with fps control; GIF export; permalinks.
- **Earth Nullschool**: minimal UI, everything in the URL, click any point for values.
- **PALEOMAP / GPlates**: paleo-DEMs; point reconstruction service; multi-model comparison (uncertainty by model).
- **Pleiades**: places with attested periods and explicit location precision.
- **DARMC**: thematic layers 0–1500 (roads, aqueducts, shipwrecks, epidemics…). **AWMC**: period water levels and ancient rivers. **ORBIS**: Roman travel time/cost.
- **Histropedia**: Wikidata timelines with "priority display" density cap.
- Recent: **HistoryMaps** (story / timeline / map views, animated battles), **ICEMAP** (icemap.no, Eurasian ice-sheet story for schools), **AADR Visualizer** (ancient-DNA individuals, filters), **Empirescope** (Cliopatria playback; unverified).

## Ranked ideas for Karahan Era (effort S/M/L)
1. **Playback** with speed + loop range, log-time speed that slows near today — S–M, no data.
2. **Compare two years**: swipe / fade / spy lens (A vs B) — M, no data.
3. **"What happened here"** place history: land/sea, ice, biome, temperature, rulers, nearby C14 dates, population, events through time for a clicked or searched point — M; GeoNames cities15000 (CC BY) for search.
4. **Search with autocomplete** over events, states, peoples, eras, places; year parsing ("12 ka") — S–M.
5. **Guided stories / tours** (year + view + layers + caption steps) — M (engine S, writing M).
6. **Full state in the URL + share card / PNG** with caption and attribution — S.
7. **GIF/WebM export** of a time span — M.
8. **Uncertainty display**: fuzzy borders, date ranges on events, location precision halos, model spread bands — M.
9. **Map ↔ chart linking**: chart range sets playback loop, hover shows ghost coastline, state area/population sparklines — M.
10. **Milestone jump menu + step buttons** (±1/10/100/1000 yr, ±1 glacial cycle, keyboard) — S.
11. **Region focus** from lane headers ↔ map — S.
12. **Timeline category filters + density cap** — S–M (needs a category tag per event).
13. **Colour states by a variable / isolate selection** (Seshat variables) — M.
14. **Sources & download panel** per layer (dataset, version, licence, DOI, what it shows / doesn't) — S.
15. Lower: AWMC rivers, ORBIS-style travel time (L), aDNA layer (M), Old Maps Online link-out (S), "guess the year" quiz (S–M).

Suggested order: 1, 10, 6 → 2, 3, 4 → 5, 8 → rest.
