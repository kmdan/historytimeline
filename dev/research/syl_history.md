# What top universities teach in intro World / Global History (survey of syllabi, Oct 2026)

Method: web search + fetch of public course pages/syllabi (no Reddit). Many elite courses (Harvard Gen Ed, Yale, Stanford, Columbia, Berkeley) do **not** publish weekly schedules publicly (Canvas-gated); for those only catalogue descriptions were available and are marked "(desc. only)". Full weekly schedules were obtained for MIT, Princeton (via Bard HI109), UCSB, UVA/Coursera, Crash Course, and period lists for Oxford/Cambridge.

Also checked, not usable: Columbia "Contemporary Civilization" is a great-books political/moral philosophy course (Plato → Fanon), not a world-history survey (https://bulletin.columbia.edu/columbia-college/core-curriculum/contemporary-civilization). Berkeley HIST 2 "Comparative World History" is a rotating theme course (cities, food, war) with no fixed syllabus (https://guide.berkeley.edu/undergraduate/departments/history). Harvard College Gen Ed has no public world-history survey syllabus.

Note: the most common shared textbook across elite surveys is *Worlds Together, Worlds Apart* (Tignor/Adelman et al., Princeton-authored) — used by B and C; its chapter list effectively is the de-facto "canon".

## 2. Recurring topics, ranked (count = of the 14 sources above; coarse, since several are desc.-only)

| Rank | Topic (count) | Typical sub-themes | Map-timeline datapoints / datasets |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Empires & imperialism, esp. New Imperialism 1870–1914 (12) | Scramble for Africa, Raj, Opium Wars, settler colonies, resistance | Colonial borders per year (historical-basemaps, Cliopatria); dates of conquest/annexation; railway & telegraph lines; famines (Late Victorian Holocausts) |
| 2 | Long-distance trade networks: Silk Roads, Indian Ocean, trans-Saharan (11) | Merchants, monsoon sailing, caravan cities, diasporas, porcelain/silk/spice | Route polylines with active periods (OWTRAD, ORBIS for Rome); ports/caravanserais; monsoon wind arrows; shipwreck finds (Belitung) |
| 3 | First cities & states / river-valley civilisations (9) | Mesopotamia, Egypt, Indus, Yellow R., Norte Chico, Mesoamerica; writing, bureaucracy | City founding dates and population (Reba et al. 2016 urban dataset, Chandler/Modelski); writing-system origins; Seshat polity data |
| 4 | Mongol Empire & Black Death / 14th-c. crisis (9) | Pax Mongolica, plague spread, post-plague recovery | Mongol extent by decade; plague arrival front (already in atlas); Little Ice Age onset; population loss estimates |
| 5 | Revolutions (Atlantic: American, French, Haitian, Latin American; 1848) (9) | Rights, nationalism, abolition | Revolution dates/locations; independence dates per country; 1848 uprisings map |

| 8 | Columbian Exchange & European "contact" with Americas (8) | Crops/animals/diseases, demographic collapse, silver | Crop/animal origin→destination with dates (Nunn & Qian 2010); population-collapse estimates (Koch et al. 2019, debated ranges); Potosí silver flows |

| 10 | Age of Exploration / maritime empires (Iberian, Dutch, English; Zheng He) (8) | Navigation, trading-post empires, VOC/EIC | Voyage tracks (Columbus, da Gama, Magellan, Zheng He) with dates; fort/factory locations; Manila galleon |

| 12 | Classical empires: Rome, Han, Persia, Maurya; comparison (7) | Roads, bureaucracy, collapse | Extents per century (Cliopatria); Roman roads (DARMC/ORBIS), Great Wall; population estimates |
| 13 | Nationalism & nation-states (19th c.) (7) | Unifications (Italy, Germany), Meiji Japan | Border changes 1815–1914; unification dates |
| 14 | Migration & diasporas (mass migration 1840–1914; earlier movements) (7) | European emigration, Asian indentured labour, Chinese diaspora | Flow arrows with counts (migration datasets, e.g. Hatton & Williamson numbers; indenture records); already covered pre-1900 in atlas |
| 15 | Human origins & early human migration (Out of Africa) (6) | Hominins, foragers, peopling of the Americas/Sahul | Fossil/site dates (already in atlas), migration routes, ice-age land bridges |
| 16 | World wars & total war (6, but post-1900) | WWI, WWII, atrocities | Fronts, casualties (outside atlas focus) |
| 17 | Islamic expansion & caliphates (6) | Umayyad/Abbasid, Islamic science, Baghdad | Extents 632–750; cities; House of Wisdom |
| 18 | Environment & climate in history (6) | Little Ice Age & 17th-c. crisis, Medieval Climate Anomaly, volcanoes | Temperature reconstructions (PAGES2k), eruptions (eVolv2k, in atlas), famine/plague events |

| 20 | Cold War & decolonisation (5, post-1900) | Independence wave | Independence dates per country (mostly after 1900) |
| 21 | Nomads vs settled states / steppe empires (5) | Xiongnu, Turks, chariots, horse domestication | Steppe empire extents; horse domestication (Librado et al. 2021) |

Gap check vs. current atlas (from CLAUDE.md): already strong on 15, 7, 4 (plague), 18 (climate/eruptions), 12/1 (Cliopatria borders), 14 (migrations). Least covered relative to how often courses teach them: **trade networks (2), Columbian Exchange (8), Atlantic slave trade (9), exploration voyages (10), religion spread (11), Industrial Revolution/Great Divergence (6), revolutions/independence dates (5).**
